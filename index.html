EMBEDDED SYSTEMS / PCB DESIGN

Autonomous Camera-Guided Vehicle

01

Developed a camera-guided autonomous vehicle for a competitive track challenge requiring the robot to remain within lane boundaries while completing three laps as quickly as possible.

Hardware Development

Designed the vehicle's custom circuit board and prepared the PCB for external manufacturing. The completed board was professionally fabricated before being integrated into the team's robot.

Software

Wrote the majority of the control software, totaling more than 1,000 lines of Python. The program incorporated lane detection, camera-data processing, steering correction, state-based decision making, and real-time navigation logic.

Engineering Challenge

The normal navigation algorithm calculated an experimental center using the two visible lane boundaries. Sharp turns occasionally caused only one lane to remain visible, making the normal center calculation temporarily impossible.

Solution

Developed fallback navigation logic that calculated the deflection angle of the remaining visible lane and temporarily guided the vehicle using that angle until both lane boundaries were visible again.

RESULT

The vehicle competed in a live three-lap track demonstration and placed 4th overall during a presentation attended by faculty, students, and visiting families.

DIGITAL INTEGRATED CIRCUIT DESIGN

8-Bit Barrel Shifter

02

Independently designed, implemented, and verified an 8-bit barrel shifter using Cadence Virtuoso, progressing from circuit-level design through physical layout and verification.

Circuit Design

Developed the barrel shifter at both the gate and transistor levels, connecting digital logic behavior with the CMOS circuitry required for physical implementation.

Schematic & Simulation

Created the complete schematic in Cadence Virtuoso and performed circuit simulations to verify expected functionality using simulated waveform analysis.

Physical Layout

Created the physical IC layout and completed design-rule checking to verify that the geometry satisfied fabrication constraints.

Verification

Completed DRC and layout-versus-schematic verification to ensure that the physical layout accurately represented the intended electrical circuit.
