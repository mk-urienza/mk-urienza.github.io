About Me

I earned my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of California, Davis and am now pursuing an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC Davis.

My primary interests lie in integrated circuit design and semiconductor engineering, particularly the relationship between device physics, transistor-level behavior, physical fabrication, digital systems, and larger hardware platforms.

My projects have taken me from designing and verifying CMOS circuits in Cadence Virtuoso to fabricating semiconductor devices in a cleanroom, programming autonomous vehicles, developing FPGA systems, and designing custom PCBs.

I enjoy engineering problems that require both theoretical understanding and physical implementation, especially when a project moves from an initial schematic or algorithm into a working hardware system.
