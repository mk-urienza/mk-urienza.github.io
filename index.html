EMBEDDED SYSTEMS

Autonomous Camera-Guided Vehicle

Developed an autonomous vehicle capable of interpreting lane markings using camera input and microcontroller-based control logic.

Engineering Challenge

The vehicle normally determined its position by calculating the center between two visible lane boundaries. During difficult turns, however, the camera could temporarily detect only one lane.

Solution

Developed fallback logic that used the deflection angle of the remaining visible lane to temporarily guide the vehicle until both lane boundaries became visible again.
